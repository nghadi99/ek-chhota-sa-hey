Ek Chhota Sa “HEY”

A little love story, made for Avantika
Some stories begin with a grand moment.
Ours began with just one little “HEY”.

And somehow… that was enough to change everything.
Our Story ❤️

Kabhi socha bhi na tha…
Ki ek chhota sa “HEY”…
Meri poori zindagi badal dega.

Office ki bheed mein ek chehra mila,
Dil ne chupke se usse apna kaha.
Nazrein mili toh waqt tham sa gaya,
Ek anjaana rishta dil mein bas gaya.

Avantika-aa…
Tu mere jeevan ka sabse haseen vardaan hai,
Tere sang hi meri duniya, mera jahaan hai.

Tu meri subah, tu meri shaam…
Meri har dhadkan mein, bas tera naam.

Jaise mandir ka diya,
Tu mere ghar ka ujaala hai.
Tere saath hi toh,
Mera har kal nirala hai.

Barah August se shuru hua safar…
Phir ek raat tumne dheere se poocha…
“Do you love me?”

“Haan… pehle din se.”

Us ek sawaal ne sab kuch badal diya.
Mere har sapne ko tera naam de diya.

Ganpati Bappa ke charnon mein bas ek prarthana hai…
Har janam mein tera saath hi paana hai.

Agar zindagi phir se likhne ka mauka mile…
Main phir wahi raasta chunoonga.
Phir wahi office…
Phir wahi muskurahat…

Aur phir…
Ek chhota sa…
“HEY…”

Mujhe phir se…
Meri Avantika mil jaayegi.

Avantika…
Meri kahaani ka sabse khoobsurat hissa…
Tum ho.
